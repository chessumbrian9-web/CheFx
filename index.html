🕯️ Candlestick Guide

Candlesticks show open, high, low and close prices for a selected period.

Bullish Candle

The closing price is above the opening price.

Bearish Candle

The closing price is below the opening price.

Doji

The opening and closing prices are close together.

Long Wick

Shows that price moved away from an area before returning.

🧠 Smart Money Concepts

Explore popular concepts used by traders studying price action.

💧 Liquidity

Liquidity broadly refers to the availability of orders in a market. Traders often watch areas around previous highs and lows.

📦 Order Blocks

An order block is a price area traders identify as potentially important based on previous price behaviour.

🟦 Fair Value Gap

A Fair Value Gap is commonly described as an imbalance visible across a sequence of candles.

⚖️ Premium & Discount

Traders sometimes divide a price range into premium and discount areas to frame where price is trading relative to that range.

🎯 BOS & CHoCH

BOS and CHoCH are commonly used to describe continuation or possible changes in market structure.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common Forex questions.

What is Forex?

Forex is the global market for exchanging currencies.

Can Forex profits be guaranteed?

No. Forex trading involves risk and there are no guaranteed profits.

What is a pip?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring price movement in many currency pairs.

What is leverage?

Leverage allows greater market exposure relative to deposited capital, but it can also increase the size of losses.

What is Smart Money Concepts?

SMC is a collection of trading concepts commonly focused on market structure, liquidity, order blocks and imbalances.

Should beginners immediately trade real money?

Beginners should first focus on education and controlled practice. Anyone considering real-money trading should understand the risks and only use money they can afford to lose.

Forex and leveraged trading involve significant financial risk and can result in losses. CHEFX is an educational platform and does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations or guaranteed trading results. Always understand the risks before using real money.
