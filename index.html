🌍 Forex Fundamentals

Important concepts every beginner should understand.

💱 Currency Pair

A Forex quote compares one currency with another, for example EUR/USD.

🔢 Pip

A pip is a commonly used unit for describing small changes in a currency pair's price.

📦 Lot Size

Lot size describes the amount of currency represented by a trade position.

↔️ Spread

The spread is the difference between the bid and ask prices.

⚡ Leverage

Leverage increases market exposure and can increase both potential gains and losses.

🛑 Stop Loss

A stop-loss can be used to define an exit level for a trade idea.

🕯️ Candlestick Learning

Learn the four basic pieces of information displayed by a candle.

Open

The price where the selected candle period began.

High

The highest price reached during the candle period.

Low

The lowest price reached during the candle period.

Close

The price where the selected candle period ended.

📊 Technical Analysis

Concepts to study when reading charts.

📈 Higher High

A swing high that forms above a previous swing high.

📉 Lower Low

A swing low that forms below a previous swing low.

🧱 Support

A price area where buying interest has previously appeared.

🚧 Resistance

A price area where selling interest has previously appeared.

💥 BOS

Break of Structure describes a break through a significant market-structure point.

🔄 CHoCH

Change of Character is commonly used to describe a possible change in market structure.
