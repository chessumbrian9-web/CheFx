🧠 Analysis Concepts

Concepts you can study while using the Market Watch charts.

💥 Break of Structure

BOS describes a break through a significant previous swing point and is commonly used when studying market structure.

🔄 CHoCH

Change of Character is a term used by some traders to describe a possible change in market structure.

📦 Order Blocks

Traders using SMC may identify specific price areas as potential order blocks based on previous price action.

🟦 Fair Value Gap

A Fair Value Gap is commonly described as an imbalance across a sequence of candles.

💧 Liquidity

Liquidity analysis often focuses on areas where orders may be concentrated, including around previous highs and lows.

⚖️ Premium & Discount

Traders sometimes divide a price range into premium and discount zones to frame where price is trading within that range.
