🕯️ Candlestick Guide

Candlesticks show the open, high, low and close of a chosen period.

Bullish Candle

Shows that the closing price was above the opening price.

Bearish Candle

Shows that the closing price was below the opening price.

Doji

Often indicates that opening and closing prices were close together.

Long Wick

A long wick can show that price moved away from an area before returning.

🧠 Smart Money Concepts

Learn common SMC concepts and how traders use them when analysing price action.

💧 Liquidity

Liquidity refers broadly to the availability of orders in a market. SMC traders often watch areas around previous highs and lows where orders may be concentrated.

📦 Order Blocks

An order block is a price area that traders identify as potentially important based on previous price action. It is commonly studied alongside market structure and liquidity.

🟦 Fair Value Gap

A Fair Value Gap (FVG) is commonly described as an imbalance visible across a sequence of candles. Traders may monitor these areas as part of their analysis.

⚖️ Premium & Discount

Traders sometimes divide a price range into premium and discount areas to help frame where price is trading relative to that range.

🎯 BOS & CHoCH

BOS and CHoCH are commonly used to describe changes or continuations in market structure. They should be studied together with the broader price context.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common Forex questions.

What is Forex?

Forex is the global market for exchanging currencies.

Can I make guaranteed money from Forex?

No. Forex trading involves risk and there are no guaranteed profits. Education and risk management are important.

What is a pip?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring price movement in many currency pairs.

What is leverage?

Leverage allows a trader to control a larger market exposure relative to the amount of capital deposited. It also increases the potential size of losses.

What is Smart Money Concepts?

SMC is a collection of trading concepts that commonly focuses on market structure, liquidity, order blocks and price imbalances.

Should beginners start with real money?

Beginners should first focus on learning and practising in a controlled environment. If someone later chooses to trade real money, they should understand the risks and only use money they can afford to lose.

Forex and leveraged trading involve significant financial risk and can result in losses. CHEFX is an educational platform and does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations or guaranteed trading results. Always understand the risks before using real money.
